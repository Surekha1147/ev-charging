    FAQ & Help

    Answers to common booking and charging questions.

    How do I book a slot?

    Choose your station, select a time, and confirm. Your booking is saved locally and instantly available on your status page.

    What is the cancellation policy?

    You can cancel any booking up to 15 minutes before start time. A cancelled slot moves to your history with a red status.

    Can I change charging speed?

    Yes — fast charging is quicker but slightly more expensive. Slow charging is ideal for overnight or low-cost sessions.

    How do I contact support?

    Use the contact form or social icons below. We provide live updates for approaching bookings and help with rescheduling.
